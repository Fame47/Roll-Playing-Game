HOW TO ROLL-PLAY

ROLL-PLAY

  1. Play 3 Hands. Each hand uses 5 regular dice plus the RPG Die.
  2. Roll 6 dice.
  3. Select at least one valid scoring die or combo.
  4. RETIRE to bank the Hand Score, or ROLL-PLAY the remaining dice.
  5. No possible score = BLIP. Lose that Hand Score.
★

SCORING

Single 1 = 100 Three 1s = 1,000 Three 2s = 200 Three 3s = 300 Three 4s = 400 Three 5s = 500 Three 6s = 600 Straight 1–2–3–4–5–6 = 1,000 Triple Double = 1,000

RPG DIE

Logo = 100 points OR WILD.

As a Wild, the logo joins any 2 matching dice to complete a three-of-a-kind. It can also complete the missing number in a Straight.

HERO DICE

Any color die is a Hero Die.

1–2 Hero Dice in a scoring combo = ×2

3+ Hero Dice in that combo = ×3
